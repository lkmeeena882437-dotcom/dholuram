FAQ

Aapke aam sawalat, saaf jawab

Join karne se pehle yeh parh lena — koi shak baaki na rahe.

Kya yeh Telegram channel sach mein free hai?

Ji haan — 100% free. Koi membership fee, course fee, ya hidden charges nahi. Aap ko kisi bhi waqt koi payment karne ki zaroorat nahi.

Kya aap buy/sell signals ya trading tips dete hain?

Nahi. Yeh ek educational community hai. Hum concepts, method aur discipline sikhate hain — hum koi personal recommendation ya investment advice nahi dete, aur kisi call ki guarantee nahi dete.

Join karne se mujhe paisa milega?

Hum koi income ka waada nahi karte. Trading mein risk hota hai aur loss bhi ho sakta hai. Hamara maqsad sirf aapki samajh aur knowledge barhana hai.

Content kis zubaan mein hota hai?

Roman Urdu aur English mein — samajhne mein asaan rehta hai. Kuch posts mein Urdu script bhi use hoti hai.

Kya yeh Shariah compliant / halal hai?

Hum ilm par focus karte hain aur satta, casino, betting ya interest-based products ko promote nahi karte. Takay yeh saaf reh sake. Kisi deeni masle par fatwa sirf ek qualified aalim hi de sakta hai — hum aapko apne scholar se mashwara karne ki talqeen karte hain.

Mujhe experience ya capital chahiye?

Zaroorat nahi. Beginner roadmap zero se shuru hota hai, aur practice ke liye aap demo account bhi use kar sakte hain — bilkul free.

Yeh sirf Muslim audience ke liye hai?

Community sab ke liye khuli hai. Content Muslim learners ko samne rakh kar likha jata hai, lekin koi bhi shakhs — jo bhi uski background ho — fakhr ke saath seekh sakta hai.

Kya mera data mehfooz rahega?

Ji haan. Hum aapka personal data bechte ya kisi ko hawalay nahi karte. Tafseel ke liye Privacy Policy parhein.

Join ke baad leave kaise karun?

Telegram par "Leave Channel" par tap karein — bas. Hum aapko track nahi karte aur koi charge nahi lagta.

Aap kya guarantee dete hain?

Sirf ek: imaandari se taleem. Trading par wapsi ka koi guarantee duniya mein nahi de sakta — aur jo de, us se door rahein.
