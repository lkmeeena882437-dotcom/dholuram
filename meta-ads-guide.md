## ❌ Ye bilkul na likhein (instant reject / account risk)

| ❌ Kabhi na likhein | Kyun |
|---|---|
| "Guaranteed profit" / "100% safe" | Misleading claims — Meta policy violation |
| "Double your money in 30 days" | Unrealistic income claim |
| "Daily 5000/- earning" / "Lakhpati ban jayein" | Unrealistic outcomes (Personal Attributes + Misleading) |
| "Free signal" / "Sure shot call" | Financial products/services policy |
| "Aapko paisa denge / job milegi" | Employment claim |
| "Loan / crypto / betting / casino / lottery" | Prohibited or restricted categories |
| "You are a loser?" / "Aap gareeb hain?" | Personal attributes — audience ko target karna |
| "Muslims only" audience targeting text | Personal attributes (religion) mention in ad copy |
| Before/after money screenshots | Misleading financial claims |
| Urgency jhooti ("sirf 2 seat bachi") | Deceptive urgency |

> **Rule of thumb:** Ad mein sirf **seekhne** ki baat karein, **kamane** ki nahi. Aur har ad mein
> ek line zaroor ho: *Educational purpose only. Not investment advice. Trading involves risk. 18+*

## 🎯 Campaign Setup (recommended)

| Setting | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Leads (agar Meta lead form) ya Traffic/Engagement (agar website par le jayein) |
| **Conversion event** | `Lead` (page par "Join Free" click par automatically fire hota hai) |
| **Placements** | Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Reels, Stories |
| **Age** | 21–45 (18+ policy ke liye safe; option ho to 21+) |
| **Gender** | All |
| **Locations** | Pakistan (ya aap ka target market) — city-wise test karein |
| **Optimization** | Link clicks / Landing page views se shuru karein |

### Creative ideas (video 15–25 sec)
- Screen recording: Telegram channel ki posts scroll karte hue — "yeh hai andar"
- Simple chart par haath se draw karke "Support aur Resistance" samjhana
- Text-on-screen + soft background music (chehra dikhana zaroori nahi)

### Pixel connection
1. `index.html` ke `<script>` mein `CONFIG.metaPixelId` daalein
2. Events Manager → **Test Events** kholein
3. Page kholkar "Join Free" dabayein → `Lead` event nazar aana chahiye
4. Landing page ke footer se chaaron legal popups ek baar kholein (reviewer bhi yahi karta hai)

## 🛡️ Policy checklist (ad submit karne se pehle)

- [ ] Landing page live hai aur wohi cheez deti hai jo ad mein likha hai
- [ ] Ad copy mein koi income/profit/earning claim nahi
- [ ] Ad copy mein koi "mehenga"/personal attribute targeting nahi (religion, financial status)
- [ ] Policy pages footer se accessible hain
- [ ] Domain Business Manager mein verified hai
- [ ] Landing page HTTPS par hai
- [ ] 18+ disclaimer ad + landing page dono par maujood hai
- [ ] Koi "guaranteed", "risk-free", "sure shot" jaisa word nahi

---

## 💡 Chhoti tips

- **1-3-5 ka rule:** 1 objective, 3 ad angles, 5 creatives ke saath start karein.
- Pehle 3 din sirf lookalike nahi — **broad + interest** test karein, phir jo jeete usko scale karein.
- Comments off na karein (trust kam hota hai), lekin auto-moderation on rakhein.
- Agar ad reject ho: appeal mein likhein ki yeh **free educational community** hai, koi financial
  product bech nahi rahe, aur landing page par clear disclaimer maujood hai.
- Rozana 1 learning post zaroor bhejein — active channel par Meta ka trust zyada hota hai.
