Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here is exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Last updated: 4 October 2026
In short: we collect only what you choose to share, we use it solely to send you free educational updates, we never sell your personal data, and you may ask us to erase it at any time.

1. Who we are

This website, together with the free Telegram community operated under the name HAJI MEMON SALMAN TRADER (“we”, “us”, “our”), provides free educational content concerning market basics, financial awareness and discipline. We are not a broker, exchange, investment adviser, financial institution or licensed firm of any kind.

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we act as the data controller in respect of your personal data.

2. Personal data you give us

  • Your name, email address and telephone or WhatsApp number — where you complete a form on this page or a Meta (Facebook/Instagram) lead form.
  • Your message or enquiry to us.
  • Preferences you choose to share, such as your preferred language (English or Roman Urdu) or your level of experience.

3. Personal data collected automatically

  • Device and browser details, operating system, language and approximate location (country or city level only).
  • Usage data, such as pages viewed, the referring link and button clicks — for example, whether the “Join Free” button was pressed.
  • Technical logs, including your IP address, recorded by our hosting provider for security and spam prevention purposes.

4. What we never ask for

We never ask for, and strongly advise you never to share: bank account or card details, national identity documents, brokerage passwords, wallet recovery phrases, one-time passcodes (OTPs) or any payment whatsoever. Our community is provided free of charge, and no person is authorised to request money on our behalf.

5. How we use your personal data

  • To send you free educational updates and community announcements, but only where you have opted in.
  • To answer your enquiries and to provide learning support.
  • To understand which content is useful, so that we may improve this page and our lessons.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising in an aggregated, privacy-respecting manner.
  • To keep the community safe from spam, fraud and impersonation.
  • To comply with applicable law and to respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.

6. Lawful basis for processing

Where data protection law requires a lawful basis, we rely upon: your consent (forms, updates and cookies); our legitimate interests (keeping this page secure, functional and free from spam, and improving our educational content); and compliance with our legal obligations.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies or local storage in order to remember your preferences and to keep this page working, together with analytics and measurement technologies that help us to understand how the page is used.

Where you arrive from a Meta advertisement, measurement tools may record anonymous events such as a page view or a button click. You may block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so will not prevent you from joining the free community.

To control how Meta uses your data for advertising purposes, please visit Meta’s Ad Preferences and Privacy Center.

8. Meta and Facebook lead data

Where you complete a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares the information you entered with us. We use it solely to send you the free community link and educational updates. It is never sold, rented or used for unrelated marketing purposes. This page and community are not part of, endorsed by, or affiliated with Meta Platforms, Inc., and Meta processes data independently under its own policies.

9. Sharing and disclosure

  • Service providers — trusted hosting, email and analytics providers who process data on our instructions and under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements — where we are required to do so by law or by valid legal process, or in order to protect the safety and rights of our members.

We do not share your personal data with other members, advertisers or data brokers.

10. How long we keep personal data

We retain personal data only for as long as it is needed. In practice, this means for as long as you remain subscribed to our updates, or for up to 24 months of inactivity. Server logs are retained for a short period for security purposes. Once data is no longer required, it is erased or anonymised.

11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request erasure of your data, or withdraw your consent;
  • object to, or request restriction of, certain processing;
  • opt out of marketing messages at any time;
  • request a portable copy of your data;
  • lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority — in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

To exercise any of these rights, please email contact@hajimemonsalmantrader.com . We will respond within one month, and we will never charge a fee for doing so.

12. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including HTTPS encryption, restricted access to personal data and data minimisation. Nevertheless, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please assist us by never sharing passwords or one-time passcodes with anyone.

13. Children’s privacy

This page and community are intended for persons aged 18 and over, and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will erase it promptly.

14. International transfers

Our service providers may store personal data on servers located in other countries. Where this occurs, we rely upon appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses and our providers’ own security and compliance commitments.

15. Third-party links and platforms

This page links to third-party platforms, including Telegram. We do not control those platforms and are not responsible for their privacy practices. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Telegram Inc. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

16. Changes to this policy

We may amend this policy from time to time. Any amendment will be published on this page together with a new “Last updated” date. Your continued use of the page following publication constitutes acceptance of the amended policy.
