1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how HAJI MEMON SALMAN TRADER ("we", "us", "our", "the Community") handles information when you visit this website (the "Site") or join our free Telegram learning community.

We are a free educational community. Our purpose is to share learning content about trading basics, risk management and market discipline. We are not a financial institution, broker, exchange, investment adviser or employment agency, and we do not sell any product or paid service.

By using the Site, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site or join the community.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us voluntarily

  • Contact information — your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and any message you send us through a form, email or Meta lead form.
  • Learning preferences — for example, which language you prefer (English or Roman Urdu) or your experience level, if you share it.
  • Question content — questions you ask about learning topics so we can respond or publish general educational replies.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser data — browser type, operating system, screen size, language and approximate location (country/city level).
  • Usage data — pages viewed, time on page, referring link and button clicks (for example, whether the "Join Free" button was clicked).
  • Technical logs — IP address and timestamps recorded by our hosting provider for security, spam prevention and abuse protection.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We never ask for and strongly advise you never to share: your bank account details, card numbers, CNIC/ID scans, brokerage login password, wallet recovery phrase, OTP codes or any payment to us. Our community is free — nobody is authorised to request money on our behalf.

We do not collect biometric data, health data, precise GPS location, financial account data, or the personal data of children.

3. How We Use Information

  • To send you educational updates, learning resources and community announcements (only if you opted in).
  • To answer your questions and provide learning support.
  • To understand which content is useful and improve the Site and our lessons.
  • To measure the performance of our advertisements in an aggregated, privacy-respecting way.
  • To keep the community safe — detect spam, fraud, impersonation and abuse.
  • To comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. Ever.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where required by law (for example under the GDPR), we process data on these bases:

  • Consent — when you submit a form, opt in to updates, or accept cookies.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the Site functional, secure and spam-free, and improving educational content.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information by law.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. We use:

  • Essential cookies / local storage — to remember your consent choice and keep the Site working.
  • Analytics cookies — to count visits and see which sections are popular (loaded only after you click "Accept").
  • Advertising / measurement cookies — if you arrive from a Meta (Facebook/Instagram) advertisement, measurement tools may record an anonymous event such as a page view or button click so that advertising is not repeated wastefully.

You can decline non-essential cookies using the banner on the Site, and you can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies does not prevent you from joining our free Telegram community.

To control how Meta uses your data for advertising, visit your Meta ad preferences or the Meta Privacy Center.

6. Meta / Facebook Data (Lead Forms & Pixel)

We may run advertisements on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram). If you complete a lead form on Meta, Meta shares the information you entered (for example your name and contact details) with us. That information is:

  • used only to share the free educational community link and learning updates;
  • never sold, rented or used for unrelated marketing;
  • deleted or anonymised when no longer needed, or sooner if you ask us to.

This Site and community are not part of, endorsed by, or affiliated with Meta Platforms, Inc. Meta independently processes data under its own privacy policies. We may use the Meta Pixel or similar measurement tools to understand ad performance; these are loaded only after you accept cookies.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We share personal information only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers — trusted hosting, email and analytics providers who process data on our instructions and under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements — if required by a valid legal process, court order or applicable law, or to protect the rights and safety of our members.
  • Business changes — if the community were ever transferred, information would remain protected under this Policy.

We do not share your data with other members, advertisers, data brokers or signal sellers.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected — typically while you remain subscribed to our updates, or up to 24 months of inactivity. Technical server logs are kept for a short period for security purposes. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

You can request earlier deletion at any time (see Section 15).

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Delete your information ("right to be forgotten");
  • Withdraw consent or object to certain processing;
  • Opt out of marketing messages at any time;
  • Request a portable copy of your data;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right, email contact@hajimemonsalmantrader.com. We respond within 30 days. You will never be charged for exercising your privacy rights, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including HTTPS encryption, restricted access to personal data, and minimal collection. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please help us by never sharing passwords or OTP codes with anyone.

11. Children's Privacy

This Site and community are intended for people aged 18 years and older. They are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. International Data Transfers

Our providers may store data on servers located in other countries. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and provider security commitments.

13. Third-Party Links and Platforms

This Site links to third-party platforms such as Telegram. We do not control those platforms and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Telegram messages are subject to Telegram's own privacy policy. We encourage you to read it. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Telegram Inc.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Any change will appear on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted on the Site or announced in the community. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
