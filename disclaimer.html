3. Risk Warning

Trading and investing in financial markets involve substantial risk. In particular:

  • You can lose some or all of your capital, and in some products (such as leveraged or derivative trading) you can lose more than you deposit.
  • Past performance, charts and examples are never a reliable indicator of future results.
  • Markets can move against you quickly due to news, volatility, liquidity gaps, technical failures, or factors outside anyone's control.
  • Only ever risk money you can genuinely afford to lose, and never borrow money to trade.
  • If a product promises "guaranteed returns", "risk-free profit" or "double your money", treat it as a warning sign.

If you feel that trading is affecting your finances, sleep, studies, work or family life, please stop and seek support.
